# Privacy Policy – Tor Panuy

**Last updated:** [date]

## 1. Who we are

1.1. Tor Panuy ("**the Platform**") is operated by [Company Name Ltd.], company no. [_________], of [registered address] ("**the Company**", "**we**").

1.2. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with and what your rights are, in accordance with the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 (including Amendment No. 13), and its regulations. It forms part of the Terms of Service.

1.3. You are under no legal obligation to give us information. You provide it voluntarily and with your consent, but without a phone number you cannot register, log in or book.

1.4. **Two roles:**
   - For your user account on the Platform, the Company is the **database controller**.
   - For the information a Business keeps about its customers (appointment history with it, blocks, customers it added itself), **the Business** is the controller and the Company acts as a **holder** processing that information on its behalf and on its instructions. Requests about such information can go to the Business or to us, and we will pass them on to the Business.

## 2. What we collect

**Information you give us:**
- phone number (used as your account identifier);
- first and last name;
- date of birth, if provided;
- reviews and ratings you post;
- waiting-list requests (service, date, preferred parts of the day);
- messages you send to support over WhatsApp or email, and our replies.

**Information created by your use:**
- appointments: Business, service, calendar, time, and status (booked, cancelled, moved, late cancellation, no-show);
- whether a Business has blocked you;
- an audit log of administrator actions;
- technical information: IP address, browser and device type, access times and server logs.

**Information a Business gives us (for Business Owners):**
- business name, category, address and map location, contact details, photos, services, prices and hours;
- details of invited team members;
- details of customers the Business added itself;
- subscription and payment details with the Company.

**What we don't collect:** customer payment details, passwords, medical information, or your device's location.

## 3. Why we use it

- verifying your phone number and logging you in;
- showing availability and booking, cancelling and moving appointments;
- sending service messages: verification codes, booking confirmations, cancellations, moves, reminders and waiting-list alerts;
- showing a Business the information it needs to serve you;
- showing Businesses and reviews to customers;
- managing Business subscriptions;
- support, troubleshooting and handling requests;
- security, and preventing fraud and misuse;
- improving the Service using aggregated, anonymous statistics;
- complying with the law.

**We do not sell personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not send advertising.** Every message we send is a service message about your account and your appointments.

## 4. Who we share it with

4.1. **The Business you book with** – your name, phone number, date of birth (if provided) and your appointment history **with that Business only**, including late cancellations and no-shows. One Business cannot see your appointments with another.

4.2. **Other users** – reviews you post are shown publicly with your first name only, or with no name if you chose to post anonymously.

4.3. **Service providers** acting for us and on our instructions, bound by confidentiality and data-security obligations:

| Provider | Purpose | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | database and server | European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) |
| Vercel | website hosting and anonymous usage measurement | USA and global locations |
| Twilio and Meta (WhatsApp) | sending verification codes and messages | USA |
| GovMap / OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) | address search during business sign-up (search text only) | Israel / European Union |

4.4. **Authorities** – where the law, a court order or a competent authority requires it, or to protect the rights of the Company, users or the public.

4.5. **Corporate change** – in a merger, acquisition or transfer of business, information will pass to the successor, provided it commits to this policy.

## 5. Transfers outside Israel

Some of our providers store or process information outside Israel, as listed above. Transfers are made in accordance with the Protection of Privacy (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad) Regulations, 5761-2001: to countries whose law ensures adequate protection (such as EU member states), or on the basis of the provider's undertaking to protect the information to a standard no lower than that required in Israel.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Active account** – for as long as the account is active.
- **Deleted account** – deletion hides the account from every interface. The information is kept to preserve Businesses' appointment history, to allow the account to be restored and to meet legal requirements, until you ask for full erasure (section 8).
- **Audit log** – one year.
- **Backups** – database backups are kept for [___] days and then cycled out. Information that was deleted or anonymised may remain in a backup until it is cycled out, and is not used for anything in the meantime.
- **Login session** – 30 days, after which you verify again.
- **Waiting list** – until the requested date has passed.
- **Business accounting records** – 7 years, as tax law requires.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable, accepted measures to secure information, in accordance with the Protection of Privacy (Data Security) Regulations, 5777-2017, including: encrypted transport (HTTPS), separation of data between Businesses at the database level, role-based access, logging of administrator actions, and storing no passwords. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that information will never be exposed through unauthorised access. In the event of a severe security incident we will report to the Privacy Protection Authority and, where needed, to affected users, as the law requires.

## 8. Your rights

8.1. **Access** – to see the information about you held in the database (section 13 of the Law).

8.2. **Correction** – to ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, incomplete, unclear or out of date (section 14 of the Law). You can also update your name directly on the Platform.

8.3. **Erasure** – to ask us to erase your identifying information. We perform an irreversible anonymisation: your name, phone number and date of birth are removed, and appointment records stay without identifying details so Businesses' records are not broken. After that the account cannot be restored, and your phone number is released for a new registration. We may keep information we are legally required to keep.

8.4. Send requests to [privacy@example.co.il]. We may ask you to confirm your identity, for example with a code sent to the phone number on the account. We will respond within 30 days.

8.5. If you are not satisfied with how we handled your request, you may contact the Privacy Protection Authority at the Israeli Ministry of Justice.

## 9. Cookies and local storage

9.1. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

9.2. We store data in your browser (local storage) that the Service needs to work: your login token, businesses you marked as favourites, and display preferences. Clearing site data in your browser logs you out.

9.3. We use Vercel Web Analytics for aggregated usage measurement. It uses no cookies and does not identify you personally.

9.4. **Links to outside services.** Some actions take you to an outside service, and only when you choose them: adding an appointment to Google Calendar sends the appointment's details to Google, and contacting support on WhatsApp goes through WhatsApp. That service's own privacy policy applies to what is shared this way.

## 10. Minors

The Platform is not intended for children under 16. A minor aged 16–18 may use it with the consent of a parent or guardian. If we learn that we collected information about a child under 16 without such consent, we will delete it.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will announce material changes on the Platform or by message to your phone before they take effect. The date of the last update appears at the top.
